# Editorial images — 16 October 2026

`public/images/editorial/` contains a new hero and category scenes for content,
development, design, marketing, media, business and compliance, plus the
coordinated security-key photo from the first image series. `other` uses the
general workbench image. `src/lib/editorialImages.ts` maps every ToolCategory.

Each scene ships as WebP at 480, 960 and 1440 px with responsive srcsets.
Catalog cards and related cards load lazily. On a tool page the illustration
sits below the working tool, immediately before its guide. Functional icons,
input controls, tool outputs and generated social cards remain code-owned.

Eight new originals were generated with the built-in image tool and visually
inspected. Sorted PNG originals, WebP copies and full generation prompts are
in `stuff/03-Materialien/Bildserie-2026-10-16/Tools/` in the workspace;
`Prompts-Shop-und-Tools.json` documents the addition. Images are decorative,
illustrative scenes, never screenshots of the application or client work.
